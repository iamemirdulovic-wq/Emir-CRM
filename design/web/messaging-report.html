How it is counted

  1. Tap · the agent presses WhatsApp or Telegram on a lead. The chat opens on their phone. The CRM never writes for them.
  2. Confirmed · back in the CRM the agent answers “What happened?”.
  3. Replied · the agent chose “He replied: interested” or “not now”.
  4. Link opened · the client opened a brochure, payment plan, location or video link.
We do not read the chats. Only taps and answers are counted.
