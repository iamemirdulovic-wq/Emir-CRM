Installed on your iPhoneNotifications are on since 3 Nov

iPhone · 5 steps

iOS 16.4 or newer · Safari only
1
Open crm.homes.me in SafariOnly Safari can install the app on an iPhone. Chrome cannot.
2
Tap the Share buttonThe square with the arrow, at the bottom of Safari.
3
Tap “Add to Home Screen”Scroll down in the list if you do not see it. Then tap Add.
4
Open homes.me from the home screenNot from Safari. Sign in with your work email.
5
Tap “Turn on notifications”, then AllowThe card is at the top of My Day.

Old iPhone? Open Settings → General → Software Update first.

Android · 4 steps

Chrome
1
Open crm.homes.me in ChromeSign in with your work email.
2
Tap ⋮ and then “Install app”On some phones it says “Add to Home screen”.
3
Open homes.me from the home screenIt opens like a normal app, without the address bar.
4
Tap “Turn on notifications”, then AllowThe card is at the top of My Day.

No alerts? Open phone Settings → Apps → homes.me → Notifications and allow them.
