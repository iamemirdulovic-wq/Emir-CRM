## What the pictures settle that the words did not

- The result strip and the "Send I tried to call you" button are **one row**,
  not two, and that button is black, not orange. Orange is for Save note,
  Change, More and Undo only.
- The three bottom-left buttons each carry a **word as well as an icon**.
- "Ctrl + Enter to save" is **shown on screen**, not just a shortcut.
- A call result in the list has **no avatar and no menu** — it is one line.
- The pinned note's background is **peach**, not white with a tag.
- The type pill on a saved note is **coloured by type**.
- `×` for the try count, not `x`.

## What the pictures do not settle

- The exact hex values. Take them from `v2-tokens.css`; introduce nothing new.
- What the quick phrases are and whether they can be configured. The five in
  the picture are the sensible default set.
- Whether "More" opens a sheet, a dropdown or a second row.
- What the phone does with a six-pill row, a chip row and four bottom
  controls at 390px. The picture is the computer.
